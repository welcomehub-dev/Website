What is Welcome Hub?

Welcome Hub is a Windows desktop application that provides a central place to access your installed applications, explore useful Windows features, and customize the application's appearance.


Instead of navigating through different places to find everyday tools, Welcome Hub brings everything together in a simple interface.

Our purpose

We believe that using your computer should feel organized, convenient, and enjoyable. Welcome Hub aims to make everyday Windows navigation easier by putting useful shortcuts and customization options in one place.
